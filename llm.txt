# SimCricketX

> A free, open-source, text-based cricket simulator and competition manager that runs in a browser.

[Website](https://simcricketx.app/) · [About and feature guide](https://simcricketx.app/about) · [Changelog](https://simcricketx.app/changelog) · [Source code](https://github.com/ManishYelam/SimCricketX)

## Match formats and play
- [Formats and playing rules](https://simcricketx.app/about#formats): T10 (10 overs), T20 (20 overs), List A (40 or 50 overs), The Hundred (100 balls, five-ball sets and ten-ball ends), First-Class (four or five days, two innings per team).
- [Match experience](https://simcricketx.app/about#play): choose teams, XIs, batting orders, venue, pitch, forecast and day/night conditions. Auto is available in every format. Manual supports T10, T20, List A and The Hundred. First-Class is Auto-only. Follow ball-by-ball commentary or use the Match Center, with scoring charts and a delivery timeline. Return to an available unfinished match from the dashboard.
- [Teams and players](https://simcricketx.app/about#teams): browse the shared pool, create private players, import CSV/JSON, override ratings, draft and publish format-specific squads, and set captaincy styles. T10 can seed from your T20 squad. The Hundred plays with your T20 squad and keeps separate match statistics.
- [Stadiums and conditions](https://simcricketx.app/about#stadiums): create a private venue or adopt an official template. Set format-specific pitch defaults and adjustments, pitch/skill blending, boundary dimensions, outfield speed and temperature. Matches retain the settings captured at creation. See the [stadium conditions guide](https://simcricketx.app/stadiums/conditions-guide).

## Simulation algorithms
- [How the algorithms work](https://simcricketx.app/about#engine): weighted delivery outcomes combine format, pitch, player ability, matchups and game state. The engine models pressure, momentum, batting intent, fielding and bowling eligibility.
- Automatic captaincy responds to score, conditions, workloads and recent play. The First-Class engine uses an innings forecast and evaluates declarations and follow-ons with dynamic programming.
- A Duckworth–Lewis Standard Edition approximation handles rain targets in limited-overs games. Professional DLS can produce different results. First-Class weather can shorten sessions through rain or bad light.
- Eligible T10, T20 and List A ties can go to Super Overs. The Hundred uses five-ball Super Fives in knockouts and retains ties in exhibition and league games.

## Competitions, records and community
- [Tournaments and tours](https://simcricketx.app/about#competitions): single or double round robin, pure knockout, single or double league plus semi-finals and final, IPL-style playoffs, and two-team custom series of one to seven matches. League-to-finals structures need at least four eligible teams. Follow fixtures, format-specific standings and playoff progression, preview and replay completed fixtures, or arrange a multi-format tour between two teams.
- [Scorecards and statistics](https://simcricketx.app/about#statistics): match history, downloadable archives, batting/bowling/fielding leaderboards, career and team records, cross-format comparisons, partnerships and head-to-head records. Filter List A by scheduled 40- or 50-over length. Export supported statistics as CSV, text or PDF, with HTML available as a PDF fallback.
- [Community and account](https://simcricketx.app/about#community): discussion threads, questions and feedback, live search, votes, images, mentions and notifications. My Profile summarizes a member's own cricket activity. Account tools cover settings, sign-in history, sessions and data export.

## Access
The About page and changelog are public. Playing, squad building, competition management, personal statistics and community participation require an account. [Register](https://simcricketx.app/register) or [sign in](https://simcricketx.app/login). Match results describe simulated cricket.
