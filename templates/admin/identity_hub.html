Adopted rules

Ordinary personal, work, school and custom domains are allowed. Known disposable domains and new plus-tag addresses are rejected. Provider-specific Gmail/googlemail aliases are canonicalized for duplicate checks; login and recovery use the stored address.

Recognized devices and exact basic fingerprints can block signup pending review. One successful signup per exact IP per rolling 24 hours; a subnet alone is not identity proof. Browser language, timezone, screen and platform are the only fingerprint signals; cookies and sightings expire 180 days after last use.

Detected VPN/proxy/Tor/datacenter networks restrict non-exempt members to Community/support. Unknown/timeouts fail open for the network rule only. Classifier cache: clean 15 minutes, flagged 5 minutes, unknown 1 minute. Private Relay feed expires after 48 hours.

Operations and retention

Schedule the documented evidence/proof cleanup, campaign processing and 30-day operation-backup cleanup. Retained closed reviews and scoped grants generally expire after 180 days; campaign receipts wait for file cleanup. Email-change and merge receipts remain durable. Minimal identity markers persist until separately authorized removal. Keep signup enforcement enabled for retired identities.

Campaign activation, expiry deletion and account merges retain their own previews and confirmation requirements. This page cannot enable them.
