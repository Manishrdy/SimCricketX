Email rules approved for future enforcement

Ordinary domains allowed; known disposable domains blocked. Reject new plus-tag addresses. Gmail/googlemail aliases are canonicalized for matching; other providers retain their own address structure.

These identity rules are not yet integrated into signup enforcement. Existing authentication continues under its current rules.

Retention and access

Evidence is retained for at most 180 days after capture and expires sooner with its underlying device evidence. Closed cases and their history expire 180 days after closure. Open cases retain minimal workflow metadata; old notes and evidence expire. Exported scan reports have a separate 30-day limit.

The operator must schedule daily cleanup. Expired content is hidden immediately. This page does not verify scheduler or backup configuration.

Later features

Signup enforcement, return approvals, VPN restrictions, rule exemptions, email changes, merges and the deletion campaign are not enabled by this workspace.
